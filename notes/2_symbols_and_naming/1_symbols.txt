1  --  Symbol names

1)  The symbols, one by one

  (a)   Brackets  --  three pairs, and they are never interchangeable

           (   )     parentheses           parens, round brackets
           [   ]     square brackets       brackets, box brackets
           {   }     curly braces          braces, curly brackets
           <   >     angle brackets        alone they are less-than and greater-than

  (b)   Quotes

           '         single quote          apostrophe, tick
           "         double quote          quote, inverted comma
           `         backtick              backquote

  (c)   Slashes and bars

           /         forward slash         slash, stroke
           \         backslash
           |         pipe                  vertical bar, bar

  (d)   Joiners and separators

           .         dot                   period, full stop, point
           ,         comma
           :         colon
           ;         semicolon
           -         hyphen                dash, minus
           _         underscore            underbar, low line
           ...       ellipsis              dot dot dot

  (e)   Maths and comparison

           +         plus
           =         equals                equal sign
           *         asterisk              star
           %         percent               percent sign
           ^         caret                 hat

  (f)   The rest of the shift row

           !         exclamation mark      bang, exclamation point
           ?         question mark         query
           @         at sign               at
           #         hash                  pound, number sign
           $         dollar sign           dollar
           &         ampersand             and sign
           ~         tilde

2)  Two-character combinations

These are read as one unit and have their own names.

     ->        arrow
     =>        fat arrow, rocket
     ==        double equals
     !=        not equal, bang equals
     &&        double ampersand
     ||        double pipe
     //        double slash
     **        double star, double asterisk
     ::        double colon
     >>        double greater-than, right shift
     <<        double less-than, left shift
     :=        walrus
     --        double dash, double hyphen
